AnytimeTreeBuilder

Windows: run the .exe installer, or extract the portable ZIP and open its .exe.
Mac: open the DMG and drag the app into Applications.
Linux: install the .deb, or make the AppImage executable and open it.
The tar.gz is an alternative to AppImage/FUSE; keep its extracted folder intact.

Choose Upload > Upload dataset to start live enumeration, or Upload graph to
open a saved graph. Data stays on your computer. Closing the window stops the
C++ engine. No browser, Python or Node.js installation is needed.

Downloads are unsigned; your OS may require first-launch approval. Linux
runtime dependencies and sandbox policies depend on your distribution.

Build instructions: https://github.com/zakk-h/AnytimeTreeBuilder#build
